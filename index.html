Our Story

Rooted in Passion,
Driven by Flavour

Food Haven was born in 2005 when chef Maria Rossi decided to transform her grandmother's kitchen recipes into a dining experience that the whole city could enjoy. What started as a 12-seat bistro has grown into one of the most beloved restaurants in town — but our heart has never changed.

We source every ingredient from local farms within 50 miles, change our menu with the seasons, and believe that great food is really about great people — the farmers, the cooks, and especially our guests.
